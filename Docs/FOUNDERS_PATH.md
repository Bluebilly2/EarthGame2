## How a "path" can exist in a game that forbids scripts

GAME_DESIGN §5–6 forbids a hand-authored quest line, and this document does not create one.
The path is authored as three things the constitution already allows:

1. **A starting goal state** — the tablet's goal planner opens with one goal ("survive
   tonight") and each goal, once met, exposes the next the way §5's dependency graph already
   works. Objectives are the knowledge system speaking, not triggers firing. *(Clarified
   2026-09-01, DEV 1's dispute 2 upheld: "exposes" means ranks to the fore, never hides —
   all goals render, ordered by `MostPressing`, met shown met; reveal-on-completion would be
   a trigger and would break the moment a met goal regresses. The goal text is the Oracle's
   own, one owner.)*
2. **A world calendar** — the founder wakes at a chosen date and place; winter deepens on the
   climate model's own schedule; the finale is a night the climate produces, not an event
   script.
3. **One gift only: the tablet.** Owner-ruled, 2026-09-01: *"the founder wakes with nothing,
   just the tablet. no clothes, no tools, no workshop, just the tablet."* The stocked workshop
   leaves the arc entirely (it survives, if at all, as a dev/test fixture). Every tool is found
   or made, which pulls one requirement forward: the beach itself must offer first materials —
   tool-stone cobbles, driftwood, reachable fibre — the old kit re-homed into the country.

The arc is therefore replayable and sandbox-true: a player who ignores the path just lives in
the simulation; the path is the lit route through it, and the tablet holds the lantern.

**What the beta is for (owner-ruled, 2026-09-01, verbatim):** *"the beta arc isnt about how
long the player survives, its about when they get to a shelter with something that resembles a
bed, a fire and some tools. the core reason for the beta arc is a proof of concept of the
game. i can show another developer what this game is, even though its not finished, and they
can get a good idea of what i am trying to build."*

**Shape of the whole, re-cut to that purpose:** wake late-winter with nothing but the tablet →
water, first stone, first fire before dark → choose ground and raise a camp from what the
country gives → the first rain proves it → **the arc completes the first night the founder
sleeps warm, in a bed they made, by a fire they lit, with tools they knapped, under a roof
they raised** — and dawn brings the tablet's summary of how nothing became a home. Three to
four founder-days: **90 minutes to two hours of real play**, dense with the game's whole
thesis. Death at any point is a full teacher — the tablet explains the mechanism, and the next
run carries the player's own knowledge forward. The winter deepening beyond that dawn — the
storms, the hunt, the solstice night — is the world the demo implies, and later versions
deliver (see *Beyond the beta*).

## Act I — The First Day (survive tonight)

*Founder-days 1 · systems: thermal, fire, water, tablet, verbs · the enemy is the night*

| Beat | The player's experience | What teaches it |
|---|---|---|
| Waking | Face-down on sand, mid-morning, empty-handed. Surf sound. No UI beyond crosshair + hands. | First look/walk/mouse verbs surface as used, not as a tutorial screen. |
| Thirst | The sea will not drink. First tablet consult: **why** (salt, and what it costs a body). | The tablet's Q&A pattern: every "no" carries its mechanism. |
| Water | The creek, 174 m inland — findable by reading the land (the gully, the green). Drink. | ECOSYSTEM's promise: country can be read. |
| First stone | Cobbles at the tideline, driftwood on the sand, fibre plants at the dune's foot. Knap an edge (the wrong stone teaches as much as the right one), cut fibre, lay cord. | The country as the only toolshop; looking at a material names what it could become. |
| The forecast | The tablet, asked about the night: tonight's minimum, and what a bare night does to a body. **This is why B1 (forecast honesty) precedes the path.** | The game's central contract: the oracle never lies. |
| Fire | Drill and hearth board carved from found wood, tinder judged by damp — the whole kit earned before dark, from nothing but hands and the tablet's answers. | Material condition (moisture, edge) as the real crafting system; fire as the first engineering project. |
| Night 1 | At the fire behind the dune: feed it, watch the stars turn, make it to dawn. | Fuel classes, the cold curve, the first dawn as the first victory. |

**Kill conditions:** cold (the canon ~03:09 death), dehydration. Both fully explained on death.

## Act II — A Camp of Your Own (days 2–5)

*Systems: shelter/thermal, food, weather v1 · the enemy is attrition*

- **Hunger arrives** as the driver: digging stick → tubers → roasting; the forest's edible
  species; pounding. The forage loop and its real yields.
- **The founder chooses ground and builds there.** This act is where the building answer the
  owner deferred **emerges as a requirement**: the path needs, at minimum — *choose any
  defensible spot* (creek-near, wind-shadowed, the player's own read of the country) and raise
  a camp from parts: **windbreak, bedding pile, fire ring anywhere legal, drying rack**. The
  existing shelter/thermal model judges every choice honestly. That is the *placeable
  components* scope, derived rather than picked; freeform stacking stays the post-beta
  horizon.
- **First weather:** a rain day (weather v1: rain, wind shifts, cold snaps driving the
  existing thermal model). Wet tinder, a tested windbreak, the first night the camp earns its
  keep.
- **Kill conditions:** cold compounded by wet; hunger's slow arithmetic.

## The arc's end (replacing the old Acts III–IV)

The completion condition is a **state the goal planner can recognise**, not a date: shelter
standing, bedding laid, fire alive, a knapped toolkit carried — and the founder sleeps a full
night in it. Dawn triggers the summary (§23's explanation tree pointed at a life): what was
found, made, learned, and what the country did meanwhile. Then, plainly: *this is the
beginning of the path; the game continues from here in future versions* — and the sandbox
stays open.

**The living world in the beta (reconciling the owner's "animals and weather both in" with the
short arc):** weather is load-bearing — the rain that tests the camp is Act II's exam. Animals
are **present, not yet hunted**: birds working the tideline, a wallaby at dawn on the plain,
tracks by the creek — the ecology's link 6 made visible, so the demo shows a world that lives
without the arc requiring hunting systems to finish. Full E5 behaviour, hunting, trapping and
preservation move to *Beyond the beta*. (FABLE's reconciliation — owner may veto.)

## What a developer must see (the demo lens the purpose demands)

Ninety minutes must carry the vision. The arc is staged so these are unmissable: **a real
place** (Seven Mile Beach as itself); **an oracle that cannot lie** (ask it anything; its
answer is the same model the world runs); **materials over recipes** (damp tinder fails and
says why; two flakes off one core are different tools); **building judged by physics** (the
windbreak on the wrong side does nothing); **a world that made itself** (valleys water cut,
plants that competed for their ground, animals the plants support). Each is a system that
already exists; the arc's job is to walk a stranger through all five without a tutorial.

## Beyond the beta (designed now, built later)

*The middle winter: hunting as engineering (days 5–11), preservation before the multi-day
storm, the goanna raiding stores, venom as a decision — and the solstice-class longest night
as the first post-beta milestone's finale.*

- **The animals arrive** (E5, NSW coast minus humanity): wallabies and a forest wallaroo as
  the meat that must be earned; shorebirds and their eggs on the flats; a goanna that raids
  stores; a snake that makes bare feet a decision; fish in the creek mouth for whoever builds
  a trap. Populations per ECOSYSTEM link 6 — what the plants support, not spawn tables.
- **Hunting is engineering, not combat:** a thrown stone teaches disappointment; a sharpened
  stick raises odds; a deadfall or fish trap (two more placeable components) converts
  patience into protein. Real knowledge — where animals drink, dawn habits — transfers.
- **Preservation before the storm:** the rack dries meat and fish; the tablet's forecast
  shows a multi-day blow coming; stores become the objective.
- **The storm:** two days of rain and wind the camp must ride out — shelter, fuel and larder
  audited by weather. Losing the larder to the goanna you never fenced is a fair story.
- **Kill conditions:** venom (decided by real behaviour, explained), cold-wet, starvation.

- **The longest night, the first post-beta finale:** a solstice-class cold snap the tablet
  names days ahead; preparation as gameplay (fuel mass, bedding depth, wall thickness, food
  buffer); hours of active fire discipline the model, not a script, decides. It was this
  document's original ending, and it keeps that role one milestone later.

## What you can do in the game today

You wake naked on a real 8 km square of the Bherwerre Peninsula, on the south shore of Jervis Bay, as it would be
if people had never existed: no roads, no buildings, nothing made. The land's shape, its beaches, lakes, creeks
and the rock under it come from real survey data, and its weather from the real records of the lighthouse at
Point Perpendicular.

- **Move:** walk, run, crouch, jump, wade, swim. Trees stop you. Your footsteps sound like whatever you are
  standing on, and the ground you cross is forest, heath, sedge, grass, swamp or sand, each where the country
  puts it.
- **Handle things:** pick up, carry, put down. Sticks and stones lie where that tree stands and that stone crops
  out, not where a spawn table says.
- **Drink:** you get thirsty on a real body's schedule and must drink. Fresh water gives; the sea refuses and
  says why.
- **Knap:** strike the stone in your hand on another and take a flake off it, by real fracture physics. A tap
  bounces; a measured hold takes a sharp small flake; a full swing takes a coarse big one.
- **Feel the cold:** a night outdoors takes heat out of you the way a real night does, and would kill you before
  dawn. It does not, yet, because a deliberate catch holds it just short of fatal until fire and shelter exist
  (your ruling, 2026-09-16).
- **The world around you:** kangaroos and oystercatchers stand about, notice you and flee. Clouds, wind, rain and
  the sun's arc run off the station's own year. The world saves itself as you play, and two people can share it
  over a network.
- **Developer's things:** F11 fills the screen, F3 opens the developer's panel in a development game, and there
  is a flight for looking around.

**Five things you can do to the world, and that is the honest list:** pick up, put down, hold in hand, drink,
knap.

## What is not there yet

No fire. No shelter, no bed, no sleep. No hunger and no food. No cordage or fibre. **No tablet** — the one object
the founder is meant to wake holding, that answers questions, is not built. No body of your own drawn below the
hands, and no hunting. And, found on 2026-09-20: creeks and streams hold water the game can judge and wade in,
but nothing draws it, so they cannot be seen.

## The path, and where along it we are

The beta ends, in your words, the first night the founder **sleeps warm, in a bed they made, by a fire they lit,
with tools they knapped**. The first act of the path runs: wake → thirst → find water → the first stone → ask the
tablet about the night → make fire → live through the first night. **Three of those seven are built** (thirst,
water, the first stone). The second act is hunger, choosing ground, and raising a camp that survives rain.

So the work between here and a game you could show someone is, in order: the creek made visible, then **fire**,
then **the tablet**, then a camp.

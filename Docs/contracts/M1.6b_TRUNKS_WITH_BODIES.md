# Contract M1.6b — Trunks with bodies

**Status:** opened 2026-09-12 on the owner's "Continue", after M1.5e, as the M1.6 draft's first slice
(`M1.6_THE_REST_OF_THE_FLORA.md`). Owner: Claude (Opus 5). The owner's lane: the feel of a forest that no longer
lets him through, and the frames.

## What was found before it was designed (2026-09-12)

- **Nothing stops a founder at a tree.** The trees stand as a layer (M1.6a) and neither collision knows a trunk
  (DEBTS, "a founder walks through every trunk"): the client casts against the Terrain collider and the props layer,
  and nothing at all is on the props layer today.
- **The drawing already places every trunk to the centimetre.** A stand tile's cell carries the tall plant and its
  height (`StandCodes`), and `StandLayout` gives the trunk its offset inside the cell from a hash of the cell's row
  and column, so the server that made the world and any client draw the same tree in the same place
  (`StandPreparation`). About 110,000 trees stand in the nine tiles a client holds round the wake, some 120 a
  hectare, so within a few metres of a body there are ones and twos, not hundreds.
- **What a trunk is drawn as is the form's.** `StandForms` gives each species a butt radius as a share of the tree's
  height, and the mesh tapers it to 0.35 of that where the crown begins (`StandMeshes`) — a number the drawing owned
  alone.
- **The server does not run the mover.** It checks a report's speed, its vertical speed and its height against the
  ground (M1.5d), and nothing else.
- **The corpus loop was laid over the ground alone.** Its legs are straight lines between waypoints; once trunks stop
  a founder, a leg that crosses one stalls the follower until it gives the waypoint up.

## What this slice promises

1. **A trunk stops a founder.** Every tree the client holds within a few metres of the body is given a capsule on the
   props layer the mover already collides with, at the radius the trunk is drawn with where a founder meets it and as
   tall as a founder can reach; a founder walked at a tree is stopped at its bark, and slides along it as they slide
   along a wall.
2. **One owner of the wood.** The taper the mesh draws with moves into `StandForms`, and both the drawing and the
   body take the trunk's radius from it: what stops a founder is the wood that is drawn.
3. **The trunks near a point** are a ClientCore list, as `LyingNear` lists what lies, held to `StandPreparation`'s
   own placement by a test.
4. **The server stays as it is,** and the decision is written down: it never runs the mover, so a trunk rule in its
   validator could only catch a client that walks through a tree, and it would correct honest founders wherever its
   idea of a trunk and the client's capsule disagreed — the defect class of CANON ruling 13, at every tree in the
   region.
5. **Seen.** A recorder scenario, `-eg-scenario trunk`, finds a tree the client holds with a clear approach, walks the
   founder into it and stops at its bark, with frames at 1440p and 1080p, and records how far from the trunk's middle
   the founder stopped against the radius it is drawn with. A walker goes round a trunk (`RouteFollower`), because a
   loop laid clear of every trunk cannot be laid at this stand's density; `lay_loop.py` keeps a waypoint out of a tree,
   and the corpus walks the loop again with the trunks solid and `join_check --only N2` green.
6. **Tests.** The trunks near a point are the trees the drawing draws, in the same places; the radius tapers as the
   mesh's rings do; a tile with no trees gives none, and a point with no tile gives none.

Pays DEBTS' "a founder walks through every trunk", on the client, with the server's part settled by promise 4.

## Non-goals

Trees felled, chopped or changed in any way (their verbs are M2's, and the stand's own diffs with them); the crown,
the limbs and the litter under a tree as bodies — a founder walks through leaves; the understorey (M1.6c); the far
forest (M1.6d); fauna.

## How it is proved

`dotnet test` and the Unity-layer compile, the count as run; `python Tools/unity/editmode.py`; the trunk scenario's
frames and its stop distance on a freshly created world; the corpus walked again on the re-laid loop with
`join_check --only N2`; the owner's hands in the forest.

## What it did (exit record, 2026-09-12)

1. **A trunk stops a founder.** Every tree the client holds within ten metres of the body is lent a capsule collider on
   the props layer (`TrunkBodies`: a pool of sixty-four, the nearest first), standing where `StandPreparation` draws its
   tree and as thick as the trunk is drawn 1.2 m up, where a founder meets it. The mover already cast against that
   layer, so nothing in the mover changed: a founder walked at a tree is stopped at its bark and slides along it.
2. **One owner of the wood.** `StandForms.TrunkTipRadiusShare` and `StandForms.TrunkRadiusAt` hold the taper the mesh
   drew with alone; `StandMeshes` now takes its rings from them, and the bodies take their radius from the same line.
3. **The trunks near a point** are `TrunksNear`, out of the stand tiles a client holds, as `LyingNear` is for what lies.
4. **The server stays as it is,** by decision (ARCHITECTURE §12): it never runs the mover, and a trunk rule in its
   validator would correct honest founders wherever its idea of a trunk and the client's capsule disagreed.
5. **A walker goes round a trunk.** The loop could not be laid clear of them: at 155 trees a hectare, with 22 per cent
   of the region's ground within a body's reach of a trunk, the first run of `lay_loop.py` with a corridor rule kept
   none of the shore, bank, cliff or platform legs out of 18,195 flat ones, and the wake's own corner of the graph
   reached neither the sea nor a bank. `RouteFollower` now steers 50° off the line when it gains no ground for 0.6 s,
   tries the other side 1.2 s later, and walks straight again the moment it gains some; `lay_loop.py` keeps a waypoint
   out of a tree, which dropped one of the loop's seventeen — it stood inside one.
6. **Tests.** The trunks near a body are the trees the drawing draws, in the same places and as thick, read out of tiles
   a server streamed; the taper is the mesh's, at the butt, where the crown begins and above it; a founder who gains no
   ground tries round what is in the way, one side and then the other, and gives no waypoint up for it.

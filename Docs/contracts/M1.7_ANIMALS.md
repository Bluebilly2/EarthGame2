# Contract M1.7 (draft) — Animals in the country

**Status:** drafted 2026-09-13 on the owner's "continue", after M1.6. Not opened: each slice opens as a contract of its
own on his word, as M1.6's did, and one question below is his to answer before the first opens. Owner: Claude
(Opus 5). The owner's lane: how the animals look, the frames, and whether the country feels lived in.

The plan (§6, item 10) states M1.7 as "Two species — eastern grey kangaroo and a shorebird — materialised from
presence, graze/rest/flee, sourced meshes; scenario `fauna`. Megafauna wait for meshes (M2+)." Its exit for the whole
of M1 asks that a stranger "sees a kangaroo at dawn and shorebirds on the tideline".

## What was found before it was drafted (2026-09-13)

- **The animals are worked out, but only as numbers.** `AnimalSpecies` holds three kinds with the densities, hours and
  group sizes `ECOSYSTEM.md` gives: the eastern grey kangaroo (mobs of eight, at dawn and dusk), the pied
  oystercatcher (pairs, by day, on the tideline) and the superb fairy-wren (family parties; the bird the dawn chorus
  needs). `AnimalCapacity` says how many the ground can feed, and world creation writes that as a layer for each kind,
  which nothing reads yet. `AnimalPresence` says where each group is at any hour: a seeded draw for every 100 m
  square, drifting within 40 m over three hours and resting outside its own hours. By its own comment nothing in it
  reacts to a founder, because an animal that fled would be state.
- **Entities can already move and be seen.** The store stamps each changed field with its tick; the server sends a
  player the entities within 1,500 m of them, takes them away past a 50 m margin, and sends their moves unreliably
  under a byte budget; the client draws a thing between the positions it was told. What an entity cannot carry yet
  is an animal's kind and pose (grazing, resting, running), and the store refuses the animal definitions as not
  spawnable.
- **Everything in the store is saved and counted in the world's digest.** An animal stood up round a founder would be
  written into the world's files and would change the digest the rejoin check (N3) compares, though presence says a
  save never carries an animal.
- **A check counts the wrong thing.** The two-player soak (N4) is meant to show that both clients agree on how many
  entities are near them; `join_check` compares how many other players each one holds. With nothing but players
  moving, the two have agreed (DEBTS, dated 2026-09-13). N3's live rejoin row likewise compares entities that have
  never moved.
- **Budgets.** §8 gives animals 1.0 ms of the client's processor and 0.6 ms of its graphics card a frame. N4 holds 95
  in 100 server updates under 5 ms and each client under 20 KB a second on average after its join; the server's
  entity budget (4,096 bytes an update, twenty updates a second) would allow about four times that.
- **How many.** At the kangaroo's ceiling of 30 a square kilometre, the 1,500 m a player is sent holds about two
  hundred, and the store's search for what is near walks every entity, for every player, every update. The first
  Earth Game stood its animals within 200 m of the founder.
- **Looks.** The plan and §8 say "sourced meshes", with simple animation. Everything this game draws so far is made
  in code, and §12 records that the project imports no mesh. A sourced mesh is a download and a licence.
- **What the first Earth Game learned.** Its animals were a function sampled near the founder: they did not see the
  founder, did not flee and could not be touched; their bodies were coloured boxes that slid with their legs still;
  its fauna check was owed and never ran; and a kept body once changed kind, a wren's box sliding onto a diprotodon
  (`Earth Game/Assets/EarthGame/Scripts/Flat/FaunaView.cs` and `FaunaBinding.cs`; its `Docs/BETA_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md`).

## The slices, in order

**M1.7a — Animals in the world.** (Opened 2026-09-13 as `M1.7a_ANIMALS_IN_THE_WORLD.md`, on William's "while i
decide, can you continue?", which holds what it promises.) The server stands up the kangaroo mobs and oystercatcher
pairs that presence puts near each founder, as entities never saved and never counted in the world's digest, and
takes them away again further off; they graze, rest and drift in their own hours, so two players are shown the same
animals in the same places. The wire carries an animal's pose (protocol version 9). Nothing is drawn. The soak counts
entities; the corpus stays green with animals round both players.

**M1.7b — Animals you can see.** The client draws them posed and moving, with simple animation, made as William
chooses below. A `fauna` scenario records a mob at dawn and a pair on the tideline, at 1440p and 1080p, and the
frame's cost is measured against §8's lines.

**M1.7c — Animals that notice you.** A founder who comes close sends a mob bounding away and a pair running or
taking off. What they do is the server's while they are stood up and is forgotten when they are taken away, so the
country's presence takes over again; two players watching see the same flight.

**Not in the beta: the rest of the peninsula's record.** William, 2026-09-13: "i feel as though making animals that are
too obscure for the beta arc is too much." The swamp wallaby, the echidna, the bandicoot and the possums wait for the
biosphere designed after the beta arc (`Docs/BIOSPHERE.md`, CANON ruling 27).

## What is William's before M1.7b opens

**How the animals look.** Either made in code, low poly like the trees and the undergrowth, with simple animation also
in code: nothing downloaded, nothing to license, and a look that matches the rest of the country. Or sourced models,
as the plan first said: a download from a free (CC0) pack, which needs his permission and a notice, in someone else's
style. Claude recommends making them in code. Whichever he picks, §8's "sourced meshes" line is rewritten to match.

What the research of 2026-09-13 found, before he answered. No pied oystercatcher model exists to buy or download,
no kangaroo on sale names a hopping animation, and no megafauna model is usable. AI model generators make neither a
hop nor a peck, and their free plans' terms do not suit a game that may be sold. Unity does not instance a skinned
mesh, so animals drawn by the dozen move best by arithmetic in the shader, as the trees are drawn, grounded in the
published measurements of how kangaroos hop and walk and how birds beat their wings.

## Non-goals

Hunting, catching or touching an animal (the beta's "present, not yet hunted" was an earlier agent's reconciliation,
not a ruling); a founder bumping into an animal; animal sounds and the dawn chorus (the fairy-wren stays a presence
function for M1.8's soundscape); tracks; the tablet's page on animals (M1.9); megafauna (M2, when they can be drawn);
animals drawn far beyond where they are stood up; the rest of the peninsula's record, and a biosphere that runs as a
whole (after the beta arc, CANON ruling 27).

## How each slice is proved

`dotnet test` and the Unity-layer compile, the count as run; `python Tools/unity/editmode.py`; the `fauna` scenario's
frames on freshly created worlds; an independent check that the animals stood up keep to their living by the world's
own layers (kangaroos on ground that feeds them and within reach of fresh water, oystercatchers on the shore strip)
and that their numbers over a walk sit inside the published density bands, both numbers printed; the corpus, N1 to
N4, green with animals round both players and the soak counting entities; the frame's cost by `-eg-hold` against §8;
the owner's eyes on the frames.

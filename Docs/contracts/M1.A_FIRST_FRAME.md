## What this slice promises

1. **Coordinates.** `Double3`, `WorldPoint`, `LocalFrame` and `GeoMath` ported from v1 into `EarthGame.Engine`
   with `CoordinateTests`. The frame is the tangent plane at the region centre and its map to latitude and
   longitude is the same small-angle map the bake used, so the raster's cell (row, column) *is* local (east,
   north) by definition and a test pins the engine to the sidecar's stated frame.
2. **The sun.** `SolarClock` ported (NOAA declination, elevation, azimuth, daylight, sunrise and sunset) with its
   tests moved to Bherwerre; a console tool prints the contracted `eg2.almanac` line for a day and a place, so the
   owner's `solar_check.py` has something independent of the tests to read.
3. **Locomotion.** Tobler's hiking function and the Pandolf cost ported unchanged with their tests; the
   gait-multiplier discrepancy the v1 audit found is recorded in `DEBTS.md`, not silently resolved.
4. **The raster loader.** `RegionRaster` reads the `eg2.raster` v1 sidecar and its `.r32`; refuses a version,
   size or checksum mismatch; a `Heightfield` samples height, slope and normal bilinearly. The loader's tests read
   a fixture written by Python and committed under `Data/fixtures/`.
5. **The mover.** `Mover.Step(state, input, dt, IWorldCollision)` as a pure function: walk, scramble and slide
   thresholds, step-up, gravity and a jump, grounded and airborne control, wading; tested on analytic collision
   worlds. `HeightfieldCollision` is the server's implementation (ground clamp and speed cap).
6. **The protocol grows** to version 2: `PlayerMove` (client → server, input and the state it produced),
   `PlayerState` (server → client) and `Correction`; every message round-trips in a test; the server validates
   speed, ground and extent and answers a violation with a `Correction` the client applies.
7. **One Terrain tile** (1 km, 1025 posts) built from the raster around the wake point, collidable, under a sun
   and sky driven by `SolarClock`; a capsule player over PhysX with a first-person camera and the Input System
   actions; a HUD with a crosshair and the local clock; a shell with new world, continue and quit, which needs
   `world.json` v1 and the solo player's resting place written at quit.
8. **The recorder.** `-eg-record <dir>` renders the main camera off-screen at 1440p and 1080p at stated instants
   and writes `run.jsonl` v1 (a header line then one record per event); the run is muted and shows no window.
9. **Frames for the owner's eyes**, and the build for the owner's hands: the mover-feel decision is his and is
   made by playing, not from frames.

## Error budget and non-goals

No flora, fauna, weather or water rendering; the sea is a flat plane at zero. Frame-rate numbers are recorded,
not claimed against the budget (that is M1.4's contract). No second player (M1.B). No save of anything but the
world identity, the clock and the solo player's resting place.

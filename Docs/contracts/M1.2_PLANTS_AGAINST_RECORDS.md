# Contract M1.2b — The plants against the records

**Status:** opened 2026-09-10 and landed the same day (the exit record is below), on the owner's "go" for three jobs in order, of which this is the second: research the
real peninsula and check the world against it (CANON ruling 21). Owner: Claude (Opus 5). The owner's lane: none —
ruling 21 makes checking the world against the real place the agent's, because "i dont really know too well".

The frames of M1.4d showed a peninsula painted by rules nobody had held against the place. This slice holds the
world's plants against where people have actually found them, fixes what the comparison says is wrong, and leaves a
check behind so the next change to the rules is held against the same records.

## What was found before it was designed

**The park's own account** (Parks Australia, booderee.gov.au, read 2026-09-10): "Dunes and sandy soils overcap the
Peninsula", and they "depend upon the presence of vegetation cover for stability" (Geology); where the soil is
deeper the ground turns to "tall blackbutt forest" (Cave Beach walks); the park is "coastal cliffs and heaths,
sandy beaches, rock platforms … swamps, lakes, forests" (Nature); the bay shore at Green Patch is "rocky shore",
"rock platform" and "tall blackbutt forest", with "woodland and heath" inland (Green Patch walks); the ocean side
is "tall eucalypt forest and tea tree towards the coast" (Steamers Beach walks).

**Four causes, the first three found in the world itself before any of it was touched, the fourth while the made
coast was proving promise 3:**

1. **Loose sand is read as deep soil.** The soil model's depth is the depth of loose material over rock: 2.3 to
   2.5 m under the beaches and 0.8 to 2.2 m under the dunes. Spinifex, the sand-binder, is held to soil under
   20 cm and can therefore win nowhere, while plants that want deep soil hold the foredune: bracken, "under the
   forest" by its own description, is half the ground within 100 m of the sea.
2. **The salt specialist wins where there is no salt.** Coast banksia, "the seaward face of the dune" by its own
   description, holds 40 % of the land at a median 996 m from the sea, and it is what the world grows at the record
   cells of blackbutt (24 of 42), bangalay (10 of 20) and old-man banksia (31 of 58). Nothing makes it lose inland.
3. **The ground's colour paints the dunes bare.** M1.4d's cascade asks the dune before the plants, so all 1,005 ha
   of dune read as bare sand, where the park says the dunes are held by what grows on them.
4. **A tree takes every cell it can stand on at all.** The community draws which tall plant holds a cell from those
   that can stand there and never draws none, so 99.9 % of the land stands under a canopy, every beach cell
   included, where the park describes heaths. Nothing that needs full sun can win anywhere a tree survives: the
   young dune goes to a coast banksia barely able to grow on it, and the ground under that to what grows in shade.
   With the soil on the sand already corrected, the made coast's sand-binder won no cell of its young dune.

## What this slice promises

1. **The records, fetched and kept.** `Tools/data/fetch_ala.py` fetches the Atlas's records of the twelve plants
   inside the box and caches them under `Data/cache/ala/` (fetched, never committed), each record with the licence
   of the resource it came from; `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` says so.
2. **A check, `species_check.py`.** For every plant with at least five usable records: its share of the ground
   within 100 m of its records against its share of all land, which must be at least one; and the median distance
   from the sea of the world's plant against its records', within a factor of two. A plant the world grows on under
   a hectare cannot carry a meaningful share, and its row asks instead that it grow within 100 m of at least half
   its records. Both numbers printed on every row. A row the world still fails after this slice is owed by name
   in DEBTS.md: it prints its numbers and "owed", and fails the check the day it passes while still owed.
   Independent of the tool by its data: records gathered by people
   in the field, against a world grown by rules that never saw them.
3. **The soil a plant reads on sand is the soil that has formed there.** On beach and dune ground the depth a plant
   reads is the soil model's, reduced where the salt wind reaches — the sand nearest the sea is bare of soil
   whatever lies under it — so the sand-binder holds the young dunes and the plants that want soil do not. The
   exposure is the world's own layer; the records then judge the result rather than set it.
4. **A salt specialist loses where the salt does not reach.** Coast banksia gains a floor on exposure, the
   counterpart of spinifex's ceiling on soil: below it the trees that need shelter win the ground.
4b. **A tree stands as often as the ground suits trees.** A tall plant stands on a cell with a chance equal to how
   well the best suited of them is suited to it, and which one is drawn as before, from a third roll so that the two
   every world already makes keep their values. Where trees barely grow the ground is open, and the heath, the grass
   and the sand-binder have the sun.
5. **The ground's colour follows what grows.** On dune ground the cover is what grows there; bare dune sand is left
   only where nothing, or only the sand-binder, grows. `cover_check.py` restates the new cascade.
6. **Where the founder wakes follows the world.** If the changes move the scorer's wake, the corpus loop is re-laid
   by a tool (`Tools/corpus/lay_loop.py`, the search M1.4's wake-point removal did by hand) and walked again.
7. **Tests** of the soil a plant reads, of the exposure floor, of how often a tree stands, and of the cascade's new
   order, each against a made world where the answer is known.
8. **Frames**: the region map before and after, the clearest picture there is of where things grow, and the three
   vantages of M1.4d. The owner has deferred judging the ground's colour until things stand on it; these are for the
   record.

## Non-goals

Fitting every plant to its records: only the four causes are fixed, and a plant still off after them is a debt
with its numbers. How much of a plant there is, which presence records cannot say. The heath's own rules: the
heath is expected to come back where the canopy opens, and that is measured here, not promised. The lakes' areas
(DEBTS.md, "lake beds are the bake's ground"). Animals.

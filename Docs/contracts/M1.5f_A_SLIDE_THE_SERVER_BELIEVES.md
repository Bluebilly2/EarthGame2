# Contract M1.5f — A slide the server believes

**Status:** closed 2026-09-14, the exit record at the end; opened the same day, on William's "continue", after the bug
hunt he asked for on 2026-09-13 found the server correcting a founder who slides down ground too steep to stand on.
Owner: Claude (Opus 5 to the tests' first red and green; the record and the commit by Fable 5.1, the model swapped
under it that day).

## What was found (2026-09-13, shown 2026-09-14)

- **A slide is corrected as a cheat.** On ground steeper than 45° the mover gives the founder to gravity, and on a 61°
  face the slide gathers about four metres a second of speed across the ground each second. The server holds every
  report to a run's ceiling, 7.04 m/s, whatever the body is doing. A test on the code as it stood, sliding down a 61°
  face and reporting as the game reports, was corrected 1.46 s in, with the body moving at 6.08 m/s. The wake lies
  within 2 km of cliffs and shore platforms.
- **A report reads a fifth fast.** The game reports every 0.05 s of fixed steps of 0.02 s, so after three steps and
  then after two, and the server judges each over one tick of 0.05 s. A report of three steps reads 20 % fast, which is
  why that slide was measured at 7.20 m/s; running at its fastest reads 6.76 m/s against the ceiling.

## What this slice promises

1. **A founder off their feet is believed as fast as a run and the height they have lost since they last stood**
   (v² = run² + 2 g drop, with the tolerance as before). A slide down a 61° face, reported as the game reports, is not
   corrected in three seconds, by which time it has outrun a run's ceiling.
2. **Height gained buys nothing.** A founder off their feet level with where they last stood, or above it, is held to a
   run, and the allowance is measured from where they stood, not from their last report, so it cannot grow report by
   report.
3. **The server keeps where each founder last stood:** the first report's height, then every report on their feet, and
   a saved founder's from their save.

## Non-goals

The fifth-fast reading, which running still passes (owed in DEBTS); the client's collision against the server's at
cliff edges, a debt of its own; how a slide feels.

## How it is proved

`dotnet test`, each new test's reason broken once; the Unity-layer compile.

## Exit record (2026-09-14)

**What exists.** `MovementValidator.HorizontalCeiling`: on their feet a founder is held to a run; off them, to a run
together with the height lost since they last stood, v² = run² + 2 g drop, height gained counting for nothing.
`PlayerSession.StoodUp` keeps that height: the first report's, then every report on their feet, and a saved
founder's from their save. What a player notices: sliding down a cliff face no longer snaps them back up it, over
and over, until the bottom.

**Tested, and how.**

- Before the fix, the slide test on the code as it stood: a body sliding down a 61° face, reported as the game reports,
  was corrected 1.46 s in at 6.08 m/s, read as 7.20 m/s against the ceiling of 7.04.
- `dotnet test`: 519 passed. The same slide runs three seconds uncorrected and ends faster than a run's ceiling. Over
  the wire on a world without terrain: a fall of two and a half metres at 10 m/s is believed and keeps where the
  founder stood; a body back level with that height at 12 m/s is corrected; standing again moves the height; a body
  two metres above it at 9 m/s is corrected. A founder who rejoins off their feet is judged from the height their
  save held.
- Each new test's reason was broken once and the tests went red: a body off its feet held to a run (all three);
  height gained buying speed; the height following every report rather than those on their feet; a saved founder's
  height forgotten. The last case's restore of `GameServer.cs` was refused by the machine on its first try (the open
  itself, "invalid argument"), the way the closing save's replace was refused on 2026-09-13; the file was put right by
  hand, the case run again with a retried restore, red as expected, and the restored code green.
- The Unity-layer compile: 0 warnings, 0 errors. No Unity-side code changed.

**Not exercised, or not holding.**

- The report's fifth-fast reading stands, owed in DEBTS: with it, a run at its fastest reads 6.76 m/s against 7.04.
- The slide was not walked in the built game, and no cliff of the corpus loop is steep enough to slide on (its cliff's
  top edge, 0.4 minutes of the loop, is walked, not slid). The steepest cliff in the box is a debt of its own.
- A slide on the client's PhysX ground against the server's heightfield was not compared; the divergence at cliff
  edges is the 2026-09-07 debt.
- A cheat that stands, steps off a height and reports a run's speed and the drop's together is believed exactly as a
  fall is; the allowance is what gravity gives and no more.

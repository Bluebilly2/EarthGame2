# Contract M1.5c — Hands and feel

**Status:** opened 2026-09-11 on the owner's "continue work", after M1.5b; the third slice of
`M1.5_HANDS_AND_VERBS.md`. Owner: Claude (Opus 5). The owner's lane: the feel, with his hands on the controls and his
ears on the sound — the only proof this slice has.

The plan's first-person feedback checklist (§4.8) is "head bob, hand sway, a punch on use, footstep dip". v1 built all
four, and footsteps by what is underfoot made from arithmetic; this slice ports them onto M1.5a's hand and the ground
the server streams.

## The rulings it must respect

Ruling 18 keeps the camera "smoothed vertically only", and ruling 12 makes the mover's feel the owner's, decided by
playing. So the dip of a footfall and the sway of a stride are laid on top of the smoothed camera, never in place of
its smoothing, and `-eg-still` takes them off again, so that the owner can play both and say which stands. Nothing of
the mover itself changes: its speeds, its step, its jump.

## What this slice promises

1. **The stride in the head.** A footfall comes from the distance walked, not from a timer: a step is 0.55 m and
   0.22 m more for each metre a second (v1's), so the steps close up on a climb because Tobler has already slowed the
   founder. Each drops the eye a couple of centimetres and springs back, and a sway of a centimetre and a half rides
   across the stride. `-eg-still` takes both off.
2. **The hand.** The thing in hand hangs off a shoulder: it takes a third of the head's pitch, walks with the stride,
   and trails the turn of the head by its weight (v1's `HandSway`).
3. **The punch on use.** The right mouse swings the hand through and back in 0.28 s (v1's strike).
4. **Footsteps by what is underfoot.** A footfall sounds of the ground the server says covers the spot — grass,
   litter, rock, sand or soil — and of water where the founder wades; louder the faster they go; four of each, feet
   alternating. Made from arithmetic at load (v1's synthesis), not recorded, and muted in every automated run. A thing
   put down lands with the sound of what it is, stone or wood.
5. **Tests.** The stride's footfalls by distance, its dip and sway bounded; every cover given a sound; the synthesis
   the same every time, inside ±1, and a sand step duller than a rock step; the hand's follow and swing.

## Non-goals

A body the founder sees (the plan's rig); the wind-up of a work verb, which M2's verbs give something to wind up;
wind, surf, birds and rain (the sound layer's own slice); the footfalls of other founders.

scenarios still passing; a walk recorded on each of several grounds, heard as the world's own cover layer names it
there; the owner's hands and ears. The gate `M1.5c` (`Tools/gate/gates.json`) runs the suites and the carrying frames.

# Contract M1.5d — Wading and the walk

**Status:** opened 2026-09-11 on the owner's "continue", after M1.5c; the fourth slice of
`M1.5_HANDS_AND_VERBS.md`. Owner: Claude (Opus 5). The owner's lane: the feel of wading, with his hands; the frames.

## What was found before it was designed (2026-09-11)

- **The server never runs the mover.** It checks a report's speed against one ceiling, its vertical speed, and its
  height against the ground (`MovementValidator`). The engine's `HeightfieldCollision` is the tests' collision, and it
  knows the sea alone; the client's `PhysxCollision` knows the sea alone too, water at the datum wherever the held
  ground is below it. The depth the server streams over each tile (M1.4b) is drawn (M1.4c) and moves nobody.
- **Water a founder can wade stands only in the lakes and the sea.** The world's surface layer gives its creeks,
  streams and swamps no depth: a creek is water by class, not by depth. The nearest lake is about 2 km from the wake;
  Lake Windermere (21 ha at 16.2 m) is 2.7 km off, a median 1.55 m deep and at most 4.5 m.
- **Within the corpus loop's 560 m of the wake** lie 185 cells the topology calls cliff, up to 52°, the nearest cliff
  top 46 m away, and 2,642 cells of shore platform, the nearest 6 m away; the sea's water is over 0.4 m deep 71 m from
  it. The steepest cliff in the box, 71°, is 2.4 km from the wake.
- **v1's K1c** drove every control through the real input seam — the hotbar by the real "2" key, the wheel stepping
  the hand and wrapping — and expected each effect, every expectation able to fail.

## What this slice promises

1. **Wading where water stands.** The client's collision reads the depth the server streams over each tile, so a
   founder wades in a lake as in the sea: slower, no running, no jumping out of the deep. Where no depth tile is held
   yet, the sea stands at the datum as before. The engine's heightfield collision takes a world's own water surface in
   place of its sea rule when it is given one, so the tests and any headless body wade by the same water. None of the
   mover's numbers changes (CANON ruling 18).
2. **The server still does not judge wading.** A founder in water is only ever slower than on land, which the speed
   ceiling already allows. A lower ceiling in water would correct a founder wherever the client's ground and the
   server's differ by centimetres at the water's edge — the defect class of ruling 13 — and would catch only a
   client that cheats, whom the ceiling already holds to a run. Recorded in ARCHITECTURE §12.
3. **A founder seen wading.** A recorder scenario, `-eg-scenario wade`: the founder, stood on a lake's shore, walks into
   the water; frames at the shore and wading, at 1440p and 1080p; the end record says whether they waded, how fast
   they went on land and in the water, and the streamed depth where they stopped. `Tools/world/wade.py` finds the
   shore from the world's own layers, stands the founder there by the host, runs the player, and prints the layer's
   depth under the founder beside what the run says.
4. **The controls scenario (v1's K1c).** `-eg-scenario controls` drives the controls asset through the Input System
   itself: a keyboard, a mouse and a gamepad are added to the running player and their keys and buttons pressed as a
   person's would be, and each effect is expected — walking, running, crouching, jumping, the look, work, use
   picking up and putting down, Tab and the gamepad's select opening and closing the carrying window, the keys 1–9,
   the wheel and the shoulders choosing the hand, the wheel wrapping round. Escape and F12, whose effects a windowless
   run withholds, are expected to reach the body's controls frame. Every check records what it expected and what it
   got, and a check whose state before the press equals the state it expects after cannot pass; the run exits 0 only
   when every check passes.
5. **The walk laid again with a cliff edge, a rock platform and the water's edge.** `lay_loop.py` lays the corpus
   loop with a leg along a cliff's top edge, a leg over a shore platform and a leg that wades into the sea, beside the
   shore and the bank; `join_check` charges corrections on all five to N2's divergence budget (ruling 13) and prints
   each. The corpus's walks are run on the new loop and the budget is measured there.

## Non-goals

Swimming, and the view from under the water (a founder walks the bed of deep water: a debt); water in creeks and
swamps, which the layers do not stand; the steepest cliff in the box, out of the loop's reach (its debt stays open);
a wading ceiling on the server (promise 2); rebinding.

## How it is proved

`dotnet test` and the Unity-layer compile, the count as run; `python Tools/unity/editmode.py`; the wade frames and
`wade.py`'s comparison on a freshly created world; the controls scenario in a built player, every check passing; the
corpus's walk and walk-solo on the new loop and `join_check --only N2`; the owner's hands in the water. The gate
`M1.5d` (`Tools/gate/gates.json`) runs all but the corpus and the last, and reads the latest corpus's N2.

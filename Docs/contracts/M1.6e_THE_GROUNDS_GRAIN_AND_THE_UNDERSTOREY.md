# M1.6e — The ground's grain and the understorey

**Status:** opened 2026-09-22 on William's "continue" after ruling 37 ("the understorey is still just a smooth green
floor with some small bits scattered around weirdly") and the three DEBTS rows it stands with: the ground has no grain
underfoot (2026-09-10), the understorey reads as a smooth floor with bits on it, and the understorey plants tufts out
on open water (both 2026-09-20). Owner: Claude. His lane: whether the ground now reads as country underfoot (ruling 37,
ruling 12, STANDARDS 6 and 15), judged on frames and then in the game.

**The one sentence:** the ground gets its own grain, and what grows on it gets its variety from the cover's own
numbers, with nothing growing where water stands.

## What was found before it was designed

- A tile's colour is one picture stretched over its kilometre at two texels a post (M1.4d), and the stock terrain
  layer cannot carry a tiled detail beside it without averaging the two (the 2026-09-10 row). The stock terrain shader
  is a full PBR lit shader with dozens of variants; the trees and the tufts are lit by `StandLit`, a Lambert with the
  sun, the sky and shadows, and the ground at smoothness 0.12 with no specular is all but a Lambert already.
- The terrain draws non-instanced (`drawInstanced = false`) with one layer given all the weight and no basemap
  distance, so a terrain shader here needs `_Splat0` and `_Splat0_ST` and nothing else of the stock's machinery.
- `Understorey.Find` places one shape a cover, at one density a cover and wetness quarter, sized 0.8 to 1.2, and never
  asks the water-depth tile the client already holds; `UnderstoreyViews` draws it instanced in the stand's material,
  which has no per-instance colour.

## Promises

1. **The ground has grain.** `EarthGame/Ground`, a terrain shader of this project's own, multiplies the tile's colour
   map by a grain made in the shader from the world position — a fine scale and a coarser one, a stated share of the
   colour's brightness, the troughs a touch darker and warmer — and tilts the normal by the grain's slope so the sun
   shows it; the grain fades out by a stated distance so the far ground stays the map's colour and never shimmers. It
   casts and receives shadows and writes depth, which the water's transparency reads. `-eg-hide grain` turns it off,
   so its cost can be parted from the rest.
2. **Nothing grows or lies where water stands.** A tuft whose foot has water over it deeper than a stated depth is not
   placed; where the water is shallower than that but present, only a sedge's clump stands, as a margin species does.
   The lake bed's sticks and cobbles are neither drawn nor reached under more than a hand of water, by one rule in
   `StandPreparation` that the drawing and `LyingNear` both ask; a tile's things are placed again when its water-depth
   tile arrives. (The rows of dark specks across Windermere that the 2026-09-20 row laid at the understorey's door are
   neither: see the exit record.)
3. **Variety from the cover's numbers.** A cover grows its own shape and a companion at a stated share (heath's shrubs
   with tussocks between, bracken's fronds with tussocks, grass with clumps, sedge with tussocks); a low herb stands
   between them on every growing cover; the tufts are sized over a wider range; and the density varies in patches
   across cells by a smooth hash of the cell grid, thick here and thin there, with the stated density as its mean.
4. **Each tuft its own shade.** The stand's shader takes a `_Vary` share and tints an instance by a hash of where it
   stands; the understorey's material sets it and the trees' leave it at zero.
5. **Counted and costed.** The census probe counts the fifth shape; the cost of the understorey and of the grain are
   each measured with `-eg-hold` against §8's 3.0 ms, as M1.6c's was.
6. **Frames** at the four vantages, 1440p and 1080p, for his eyes, and the lake vantage's frame with no tuft on the
   open water.

## Non-goals

A detail texture from a file (the project imports no picture); grass that moves; the trees' look; the creek's drawn
width (its own row); the litter and the cobbles.

## How it is proved

- **Headless, red first:** no tuft where a depth tile says the water is deep, clumps alone where it is shallow; no stick
  or cobble from a cell under a metre of water and all of them from a dry one, everything as before with no depth held; the
  companion's share and the herbs counted over a field of one cover, inside tolerance; the density in patches (the
  thickest cell of a field at least half again the thinnest) with the mean held; the same tufts every time; M1.6c's
  tests standing.
- **Sabotage:** the water rule taken out and its test seen red; the file put back byte for byte.
- **The built player:** the four vantages' frames and census, the cost with and without the understorey and with and
  without the grain, the Windermere frame's open water bare.

## Exit record (2026-09-22)

**What exists.** `EarthGame/Ground` draws every terrain tile: the tile's colour map times a grain of three scales (0.12,
0.4 and 1.7 m; the finest ridged), each scale shown only while the pixel's footprint on the ground is small against it,
the whole faded by 110 m, the normal tilted by the grain's slope; `-eg-hide grain` skips the grain's work. `Understorey`
grows a companion shape at a share between a cover's own, herbs on every growing cover, densities in patches five cells
wide, sizes from six tenths to one and a half, and nothing where the water-depth tile says water stands save a sedge's
clump in a hand of it; `StandPreparation` and `LyingNear` neither draw nor reach a stick or cobble under more than a
hand of water, and the stand is placed again when a tile's depth arrives. Each tuft takes its own shade (`_Vary`).

**Tested, and how.**

- `dotnet test`: 758 passed (`UnderstoreyVarietyTests`, the stand's `NothingLiesWhereWaterStands`, M1.6c's shape test
  widened to companions and herbs). **Sabotage:** the tufts' water rule made always-true, its two tests red, the file put
  back byte for byte. The Unity-shaped compile green.
- **Frames** on the harness build (M1.6e bcaf3f249+dirty, built 2026-09-22T05:57:26Z), 1440p and 1080p at the four
  vantages, every player exit 0 with no errors: `Artefacts/frames/vantages-20260922T055727Z`. The census of what was

  228/409/404/1041/3223; new wake 94/828/86/401/2818. Placing costs the main thread 0.9 to 1.5 ms once every six metres
  (0.3 to 0.5 before the herbs).
- **The cost**, `-eg-hold 20` at the new wake and beside Windermere, two passes in opposite orders: the understorey
  5.0/5.2 ms against 4.4/4.6 hidden at the new wake and 5.9/6.2 against 5.5/5.4 beside Windermere, so **0.4 to 0.6 ms**

**What was found on the way.** The first grain, two soft scales, read as blotches, not grain; a ridged finest scale
and a fade by the pixel's footprint made it grain and keeps it from shimmering. The 2026-09-20 row's diagnosis was
wrong twice over: the specks were neither tufts nor sticks, and only three runs with things hidden said so.

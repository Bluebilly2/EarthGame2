# Contract M1.6 (the rest) — Trunks, the understorey and the far forest

**Status:** drafted 2026-09-11 while M1.5d's corpus walked, on the owner's "what can you be doing please?". Not
opened: each slice opens as a contract of its own on his word, as M1.5's did. Owner: Claude (Opus 5). The owner's
lane: the frames, and the feel of a forest a founder can no longer walk through.

The plan (§6, item 9) states M1.6 as "Trees (entities in the interest radius), grass and litter (instances) from the
community layer; budgets measured; the census probe". M1.6a stood the trees and laid the litter, as layers rather
than entities (`M1.6_WHAT_STANDS_AND_LIES.md`, which holds that decision and its numbers). The understorey, the
census probe, and two of the debts M1.6a left are what is still owed.

## What was found before it was drafted (2026-09-11)

- **Nothing stops a founder at a tree.** Neither collision knows a trunk (DEBTS, "a founder walks through every
  trunk"). The server does not run the mover — M1.5d found it checks a report's speed and its height against the
  ground, nothing more — so a trunk is the client's collision's to know. What a trunk is drawn as is the form's
  (`StandForms`: a species' trunk radius as a share of the tree's height), one owner for what is drawn and for what
  would stop a founder.
- **The corpus loop walks through trees.** Its straight legs were laid over the ground alone; once trunks stop a
  founder, a leg that crosses one stalls the follower until it gives the waypoint up (30 s), so the loop must be laid
  clear of them.
- **The client already knows what grows underfoot.** Each 4 m post of the cover tile (M1.4d) names heath, bracken,
  sedge, grass, forest floor or bare earth, with the quarter of the land's wetness beside it: the understorey's shape
  and its vigour, without a new layer. What it does not carry is which plant of a shape: a grass tree and a heath
  banksia are both heath, lomandra and saw-sedge both sedge, kangaroo grass and spinifex both grass.
- **v1 drew the understorey** as tufts baked into one mesh a 16 m cell within 70 m of the founder, in its own shapes —
  a tussock, a lomandra clump, a bracken frond, a low rosette — denser where the soil was deeper and the ground
  gentler, taller where it was wetter (`GrassField`). It baked because its instanced draw drew nothing; here M1.6a's
  material asset has made instanced drawing work.
- **The budget.** ARCHITECTURE §8 gives grass and the understorey 3.0 ms of the frame; the trees already take 4.4 ms of
  their 2.0 beside Windermere, measured without a window (DEBTS).
- **The forest ends in a line.** Trees are drawn from the nine stand tiles round the founder, so none stands beyond
  about 1.5 km (DEBTS, "no tree stands beyond the nine tiles").

## The slices, in order

**M1.6b — Trunks with bodies.** (Opened 2026-09-12 as `M1.6b_TRUNKS_WITH_BODIES.md`, which holds what it promised
and what it did.) The client's collision gets the trunks round the founder: a few capsules, set each
frame from the stand tiles it holds within some metres of the body, each the drawn trunk's radius and placed where the
drawing places its tree (`StandLayout`); a ClientCore list of the trunks near a point, as `LyingNear` lists what
lies, held to the drawing by a test. The server stays as it is. `lay_loop.py` lays the corpus loop clear of trunks,
the corpus is walked again, and a scenario walks the founder into a trunk and stops them at its bark, with frames.
Pays "a founder walks through every trunk".

**M1.6c — The understorey.** Shrubs, sedge, bracken and grass drawn near the founder from the cover code the client
already holds, in v1's shapes made this country's (a heath shrub, a lomandra clump, a bracken frond, a tussock), their
density and height from the cover and its wetness quarter, instanced in the stand's material out to a distance the
budget decides: the frame's cost against §8's 3.0 ms, measured as M1.6a's was (the recorder's `-eg-hold` on freshly
created worlds, each configuration the faster of two passes). The census probe: what the drawn understorey holds
round a vantage, counted against what the layer says grows there. If the grass tree must stand apart from the heath,
a tile of understorey codes like the stand's is the cost, stated then. Frames at M1.6a's four vantages.

**M1.6d — The far forest.** Trees beyond the nine tiles, drawn from something coarser than the stand: the canopy's
share of each coarse cell, streamed once with the region's ring, drawn as the far band's trees are, so the forest
does not stop at a line. Its frame cost stated beside the trees'. Pays "no tree stands beyond the nine tiles".

## Non-goals

Trees felled, and the stand's own diffs; fibre and driftwood as things that lie (M2's fibre and cordage); wind in
the crowns; the stem-density benchmark still to be read (DEBTS); fauna (M1.7).

## How each slice is proved

`dotnet test` and the Unity-layer compile, the count as run; `python Tools/unity/editmode.py`; frames at 1440p and
1080p; the frame's cost by `-eg-hold` on freshly created worlds; for M1.6b the corpus's walks on the re-laid loop and
`join_check`, and a founder stopped at a trunk; the owner's eyes on the frames and his hands in the forest.

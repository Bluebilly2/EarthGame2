# Contract M1.5e — Swimming, and flight for development

**Status:** opened 2026-09-11 on the owner's word, after M1.5d: "swimming needs implementing, and when we have a player
model and character, animation too" (CANON ruling 24), and, while it was being read for, "also add flight i can use
in dev". Owner: Claude (Opus 5). The owner's lane: the feel of swimming, with his hands in deep water.

## What was found before it was designed (2026-09-11)

- **Water over the head was walked on its bed.** The mover knew wading and a deep-wading slowdown and nothing deeper,
  so a founder walked the bed of the sea and of Lake Windermere and the camera went under a surface drawn from above
  alone (DEBTS, "water over the head").
- **Nothing on the server stands in the way.** The server does not run the mover; it checks a report's speed, its
  vertical speed and its height against the ground. A swimmer is off the ground and above the bed, slower than a
  walker, and passes those checks as they stand.
- **Published paces.** Sugiyama and Katamoto held six college swimmers in a flume to steady breaststroke at 0.3, 0.5
  and 0.7 m/s and front crawl at 0.3 to 0.9 m/s, their oxygen uptake rising more steeply in the breaststroke, the two
  curves crossing at 0.49 m/s (Annals of Physiological Anthropology 11: 635–640, 1992).
- **v1's developer's flight** was a key (F) that took the founder off the ground and through it: where the view
  pointed, at 25 m/s and six times that when running, jump rising and Ctrl sinking, every rule of the body set aside.

## What this slice promises

1. **Swimming (ruling 24).** Water deeper than a standing founder can keep their eyes out of is swum. The body floats
   with its feet hanging under the surface so that the eye rides a hand's breadth above it; it swims the breaststroke
   at 0.7 m/s and the crawl at 0.9 m/s when pushed, both under the body's state as walking is; it cannot jump out of
   the water or crouch in it; a fall into deep water is taken by the water before the eye goes under; and where the
   bottom comes back within reach the founder stands and wades out. Swimming is worked out every step from where the
   body is and the water there, so no message, save or digest changes. The numbers of the float are the owner's to
   feel (CANON ruling 12).
2. **Strokes heard.** A stroke sounds as each is taken, made from arithmetic as the footsteps are, and a fall into
   the water splashes.
3. **Flight for development.** With `-eg-dev`, the controls asset's Fly action (F at the desk) lifts the founder off
   the ground as v1's did: where the view points, jump rising and crouch sinking, 25 m/s and six times that running,
   never under the ground; pressed again, the founder falls. A SOLO game started with `-eg-dev` runs its server with
   flight allowed, a host allows it with `+server.dev 1`, and any other server corrects a flying founder back.
4. **Seen.** A recorder scenario, `-eg-scenario swim`, walks the founder from Lake Windermere's shore into water
   deep enough to swim (`Tools/world/wade.py --swim`), with frames at the shore and swimming, and holds the layers'
   depth where the founder stopped against the swimming depth; the controls scenario, run with `-eg-dev`, checks the
   fly key.
5. **Tests.** Deep water swum at the float line with the eye above the surface, at the paces, with no jump; a swimmer
   reaching a shore standing and wading out, never below the bed; a fall into deep water never taking the eye under;
   flight going where it is pointed and never under the ground; a flying report let through by a development
   server's rules and corrected by any other's.

## Non-goals

Diving and the view from under the water; what swimming costs the body (the physiology's, M2); the animation ruling
24 asks for once the founder has a body; currents, waves and tides; flight for anyone but a developer.

## How it is proved

`dotnet test` and the Unity-layer compile, the count as run; `python Tools/unity/editmode.py`; the swim frames and
`wade.py`'s comparison on a freshly created world; the controls scenario with the fly check; the owner's hands in
deep water.

## What it did (exit record, 2026-09-12)

1. **Swimming.** Water deeper than 1.53 m — the standing eye's 1.65 m less the 0.12 m a swimmer's eye rides over the
   water — is swum (`Mover`): the feet hang at that depth under the surface, the water turns the body's own rise or
   fall towards that line (3 per second of the distance off it, eased at 4 per second), the founder swims at 0.7 m/s,
   or 0.9 m/s pushed, each under the body's capacity as walking is (`Locomotion.SwimmingSpeedMs`), and neither jumps
   nor crouches. A fall into deep water is stopped 8 cm under the line, so the eye stays 4 cm clear. Two rules came
   out of building it: a body off the ground looks for a bottom from its feet down to the float line, not from the
   surface, so that a bank beside a swimmer is not taken for ground under them; and a swimmer's feet are left on a
   bottom that rises under them within a step, which the shore test found (its first run ended a step 0.6 mm under
   the bed). A founder whose crouched eye would be under the water, over 0.98 m of it, stands. No message, save or
   digest changed: whether a body swims is worked out each step (ARCHITECTURE §12).
2. **Strokes heard.** A stroke every 1.1 m swum, the arms alternating, each dipping the eye 3 cm (`Stride`); a wash
   made in code that swells over its first eighth of a second and dies over half a second (`FootstepSynth.Stroke`),
   half as loud again as a footfall at the same speed (`Loudness.Stroke01`); a fall into the water splashes as a
   landing does. These numbers are chosen, and are the owner's ears' and hands'.
3. **Flight.** With `-eg-dev` the controls asset's Fly action (F at the desk, nothing on the gamepad) takes the founder
   off the ground (`Flight`, v1's numbers): 25 m/s where the view points, six times that running, eased at 4 per
   second, the jump held rising and the crouch sinking, 5 cm over the ground at the lowest — through it since the owner
   ruled the same day (below); pressed again, the founder falls from where they are. A SOLO game started with
   `-eg-dev` runs its server with `MovementRules.AllowFlight`, a host takes `+server.dev 1`, and any other server
   corrects a flight back. The HUD's line says "swimming" and "flying".
4. **Seen.** `-eg-scenario swim`, run by `Tools/world/wade.py --swim`, with the frames "swim-shore" and "swimming";
   `Tools/world/carry.py --dev` runs the controls scenario as a development game, which then flies the fly key.
5. **Tests.** Deep water swum at the float line with the eye above it, at the paces, with no jump or crouch; a swimmer
   standing where the bottom comes back and wading out, never under the bed; a fall into deep water never taking the
   eye under; no crouching where the crouched eye would go under; flight where the view points, at its speeds,
   rising, sinking and stopping, and never under the ground; a flight let stand by a development server and corrected
   by any other; strokes by the distance swum, a splash from a fall and none from walking in; a stroke's swell.

## Changed on the owner's word (2026-09-12)

Flight was held five centimetres over the ground; told so, the owner ruled "dev flight should be toggleable noclip"
(CANON ruling 25). The fly key now turns on a flight that passes through the ground as through the air: `Flight` holds
the body to nothing, and a flight that ends under the ground sets the founder on it, since the mover finds no ground to
land on under the terrain and a development server, holding no ground rule, would never correct them (ARCHITECTURE
§12). The controls scenario, run as a development game, sinks the founder through the ground with the crouch held and
brings them back with the fly key.

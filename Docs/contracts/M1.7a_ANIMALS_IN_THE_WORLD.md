## What was found

See the draft's own section. These are the facts this slice rests on:
- `AnimalPresence` places groups by a seeded draw for each 100 m square, from a single capacity it is handed.
- The world folder holds a capacity layer for each kind, which nothing reads.
- The store gives every entity an id from one counter, and saves and digests every entity.
- A client draws any definition it has no look for as a magenta cube.
- The soak's count row counts players, and the rejoin's mirror row compares players' bodies (DEBTS, dated
  2026-09-13).
- N4 holds each client to 20 KB a second on average after its join, and 95 in 100 server updates to 5 ms.

## What this slice promises

1. **Stood up where presence puts them.** The server stands up the kangaroo mobs and oystercatcher pairs that
   presence puts within 500 m of any founder, each member as an entity, once a second.
   - It takes them away when no founder is within 550 m.
   - Presence reads each 100 m square's capacity as the mean of the world's own capacity layer over it, worked out
     once when the world loads. The draw asked with a single capacity keeps every draw it made.
   - The fairy-wren is not stood up; it is heard, not seen, and waits for M1.8's soundscape.
2. **Never state.** An animal is transient.
   - Its id comes from a reserved range (the top bit set), made from its kind, its square and its place in the group,
     so the same animal stood up again has the same id.
   - It never moves the store's next id, never enters a save, and never enters the world's digest. A world saved
     with animals round its founders reads back identical to one saved without them.
3. **Where an animal can stand.**
   - A member stands on the ground, never in water: its drift is shortened towards its group's place until the ground
     is dry. A group whose own place is wet is not stood up.
   - Members of a group keep their places round the group, and face along its drift with a turn of their own.
   - Each is resting below an activity of 0.25 and grazing above it, so a mob is up at dawn and dusk and lying up at
     noon.
4. **On the wire (protocol 9).** An entity's spawn can carry an animal's pose, and a state can carry a changed pose.
   - An animal taken away is gone as having left, never as having died.
   - The Welcome carries the server's interest radius, so a client can count what it and another player both hold.
5. **Nothing drawn yet.** A client mirrors every animal it is sent and draws none: no magenta cube, nothing for the
   crosshair to meet. M1.7b draws them.
6. **The checks count what their criteria name.**
   - The soak's `sample` records how many held entities lie inside both its founder's and the other player's
     interest radius, and N4's count row reads that instead of the other players.
   - The rejoin's mirror row, which still compares bodies, stays a debt until an entity mirror row can be compared at
     a common tick.
7. **Kept to their living, checked apart from the code.**
   - The host records the animals it holds every ten seconds: kind, place and pose.
   - A new check, `fauna_check.py`, holds every one against the world folder's own layers, with both numbers printed:
     none on water, every oystercatcher within the shore strip, every kangaroo within reach of fresh water.
   - It also compares how many stood round a walk with how many the capacity layers say should stand there.
8. **Within the budgets.** On the corpus with animals round both players:
   - N1 to N4 are green.
   - Each client averages under 20 KB a second after its join.
   - 95 in 100 server updates finish within 5 ms.
   - What standing them up costs an update is measured.
9. **Tests.**
   - Presence from per-square capacity, and the old draw unchanged.
   - Transient ids stable and outside the counter.
   - Saves and digests unmoved by animals.
   - No member in water.
   - Poses by the hour.
   - Taking away beyond the margin.
   - Two clients shown the same animals, with the mirror naming what the server names once they are still.
   - The wire round trip of pose and radius.

## What it did (exit record, 2026-09-13)

1. **Stood up where presence puts them.** `AnimalStandUp`, the fast tick's second system after `ItemFall`, refreshes
   the animals on the first step of each second (`AnimalStandUp.RefreshesAt`). For every founder the world holds (its
   interest points, one for each session with a body) it asks presence for the kangaroo's and the oystercatcher's groups
   within 550 m: a group whose place is within 500 m of a founder is stood up member by member, one already standing is
   moved to where presence has it now, and one no founder is within 550 m of is taken away. Each 100 m square is drawn at
   the mean of the world's own capacity layer over the cells whose centres lie in it (`CapacitySquares`), worked out when
   a world is created or restored (`WorldPreparation`), the layer's name owned by `WorldCreation.CapacityLayer` and its
   unit by `CapacitySquares.Unit`; presence draws each square at its own capacity, and the draw asked with one capacity
   keeps every group it drew before (`AnimalPresence.Near`). The fairy-wren is not stood up (`AnimalStandUp.Seen`).
2. **Never state.** A member's id is of the reserved range: the top bit, the kind in the next seven bits, the square's
   east and north indices in twenty-four bits each, and the member in the low eight (`AnimalStandUp.IdOf`), so the same
   animal stood up again has the same id. The store holds animals apart from the world's own (`EntityStore.StandUp`,
   `Transient`): they never move the next id, the save writes the world's own alone, and the world's digest never names
   them. A world saved with animals round its founder is byte for byte the world saved without them.
3. **Where an animal can stand.** A member stands round its group's place, at a place of its own within five metres
   times the root of the group's size (`MemberSpacingM`, a looseness for M1.7b's eyes), facing the way the group wanders
   (`AnimalSighting.HeadingDeg`) with a turn of its own of up to 40° either way, both drawn from a hash of its id and the
   world's seed. Its place is moved back towards the group's own place an eighth of the way at a time until the ground is
   dry: on the region's ground, not on a cell the water layer calls a creek, a stream, a lake or the sea, and not where the
   world's water surface stands above its ground. A group whose own place is wet, or off the region, is not stood up. An
   animal is resting below an activity of 0.25 and grazing from it, so a mob lies up at noon and is up at sunset, and a
   change of pose is stamped with the tick it was made in.
4. **On the wire (protocol 9).** An entity's spawn carries an animal's pose as component bit 2, and unknown component
   bits are refused; a state carries a changed pose as field 8, and goes reliably, as one carrying an item's rest does
   (see what changed on the way); an animal taken away is gone as having left; the Welcome carries the server's interest
   radius (the protocol itself in commit 891acd9).
5. **Nothing drawn yet.** A client mirrors every animal it is sent, and `EntityViews` passes over it: no cube, and
   nothing for the crosshair to meet.
6. **The checks count what their criteria name.** The soak's `sample` carries `entities_shared`, the entities a client
   holds inside its founder's interest radius and every other player's it holds, and N4's count row compares those. The
   row pairs the two clients by the server's second rather than the wall clock (see what changed on the way). The
   rejoin's mirror row still compares bodies, and is owed (DEBTS, dated 2026-09-13).
7. **Kept to their living, checked apart from the code.** The host's `memory` record adds the animals held; a `fauna`
   record every ten seconds names the founders and every animal's id, key, place and pose; the minute's `ticks` record
   keeps the updates that stood animals up apart from the rest. `fauna_check.py` holds every place logged against the
   world folder's own layers — none on open water, none under the water's surface, every oystercatcher on the shore strip,
   every kangaroo within reach of fresh water — and, for each kind, the groups stood in the squares wholly within reach
   against the Bernoulli sum the capacity layers give, within three standard deviations and one group.
8. **Within the budgets.** The corpus ran on a world it created, with a player built from these sources and the Release
   host rebuilt from them (`Artefacts/corpus/20260913-142353`): every scenario answered, and `join_check` passed all
   29 rows. N4's soak held up to 14 animals round the two players, and both clients agreed on the entities they shared
   in all 1,794 shared server seconds; each client averaged 1,733 bytes a second after its join, against 20,480; none of
   36,207 server updates took longer than 50 ms, and the worst minute's 95th percentile was 0.15 ms, against 5 ms. The
   updates that stood animals up took 0.161 ms on average across the soak, against 0.038 ms for the rest (1,810
   updates against 34,397): standing the animals up costs an update about an eighth of a millisecond. The first
   minute's warm-up put its stand-up updates' 95th percentile at 1.50 ms, and no later minute's above 0.48 ms.
9. **Tests.** Presence from a square's own capacity and the single-capacity draw unchanged, and a group's square, own
   place and heading (`AnimalSquareTests`); the squares' means and refusals (`CapacitySquaresTests`); ids of the reserved
   range outside the count, retired as having left, never in the world's digest (`TransientEntityTests`); every group near
   a founder stood up and no other kind, the same ids in the same places when walked back to, no member in the lake and
   no group whose own place is wet, a mob lying up at noon and up at sunset, the 500 m and 550 m radii, the cadence, a standing animal moved every second even to where it already stands, and
   ids made from kind, square and member (`AnimalStandUpTests`); the squares a created and a restored world feed against
   its layers read by hand (`WorldPreparationTests`); a world saved with animals byte for byte the world saved without them
   (`RegionSaveTests`); two clients shown the same animals, the mirror naming what the server names, the animals a
   founder leaves behind told as having left, and a change of pose arriving though every unreliable message the server
   sends is lost (`EntitySessionTests`, through the tests' own `DeliveryRecorder`); the wire round trip of pose and radius
   (`EntityWireTests`, `MessageRoundTripTests`); the systems' order (`TieredTickTests`).

tests, so a capacity layer is held to its unit instead. The first water test walked its founders close enough to the
region's edge to reach squares whose own place lay beyond it, which the stand-up rightly passes over; the test's founders
now keep inside. The first quick soak parted the two clients' counts in 3 of 86 seconds (96.51 %) though every count

rebuilt from these sources before the soaks ran.

Reading the slice back while the corpus ran found that a change of pose went to its viewers unreliably, as a position

# M1.5j — A foot on that ground

**Status:** opened 2026-09-22 on William's "continue" after ruling 37 and the footstep row it raised in DEBTS. Owner:
Claude. His lane: whether a footstep sounds like a foot meeting that ground (ruling 37, ruling 12, STANDARDS 6 and 15),
judged by his ears on sounds sent to him, and then in the game.

**The one sentence:** a footstep is an impact and the ground's own answer to it, not noise shaped by a filter.

## What was wrong

- M1.5c ported v1's synthesis: every step is one burst of one-pole-filtered noise under a falling envelope, with a
  damped tone for rock. The grounds differ by brightness and length, which is why William heard them change and heard
  every one of them as "a static sounding noise" (ruling 37).
- Heath sounded as litter did: the ear's grouping of covers into sounds put heath with the forest floor, a first guess
  the exit record said was for his ears.

## Promises

1. **A step is two strikes.** The heel comes down and the forefoot follows it, 70 to 110 ms later by the variant; each
   strike is an impact, and the sound is the ground answering each. `FootstepSynth.Step` keeps its name and shape, so
   `Sounds` and the stride change nothing.
2. **Each ground answers in its own way.** Three answers mixed by the ground's numbers: a body (a damped low tone, the
   thump of the foot's weight), modes (damped tones at a hard ground's own pitches, the knock and ring of rock), and
   grains (many small impacts scattered after each strike, dense and dull on sand, fewer and crisper on litter, sparse
   loud snaps on heath's twigs). Grass is a swish: blades brushed aside, with a soft body and no grains. A wading step
   is a splash: a burst, a wash, and bubbles that chirp.
3. **Heath is its own sound.** `FootingSound.Heath` joins the six; the forest floor stays litter. A run's `heard`
   field names it as it names the others.
4. **The same sound every time, and quiet.** Every step is made from whole numbers, the same in every build; its peak
   lies inside a stated band so no ground is loud or faint by accident, and the stride's loudness by speed stands.
5. **A tool for the ears.** `Engine/tools/EarthGame.Sounds` writes each ground's steps, a walk of ten on each ground,
   the strokes and the landings as WAV files, and a blind set lettered A to G with the key kept apart, so William can
   name the grounds without being told which is which.

## Non-goals

Any other sound (wind, surf, birds, rain); the loudness law; recorded samples; a sound that changes with the founder's
weight or load; the animals' sounds.

## How it is proved

- **Headless, red first** (`FootstepImpactTests`): every step's envelope shows the second strike between 60 and 130 ms
  at a third or more of the first; a rock step's tail rings at its lowest mode where a sand step's does not
  (autocorrelation at the mode's period); a heath step has at least eight sharp transients and a sand step fewer than
  four; the splash outlasts every dry step and its wash is not silent at a fifth of a second; heath has its own sound,
  different from litter's; every peak lies inside the band. M1.5c's tests stand: the same every time, sand duller than
  rock, soil duller than grass, faded to nothing.
- **Sabotage:** the grains taken out of heath and the heath test seen red; the file put back byte for byte.
- **The Unity-shaped compile** and the controls scenario, unchanged and green.
- **His ears:** the blind set sent to him; the row is paid when he names sand, litter, heath and water without a key.

## Exit record (2026-09-22)

**What exists.** `FootstepSynth.Step` makes a step as two strikes, the heel's and the forefoot's 70 to 110 ms after it
by the variant, each answered by the ground's numbers: a body, modes, grains; twigs snapping under the whole step on
heath, a swish on grass, a burst, a wash and bubbles for a wading step. `FootingSound.Heath` is the seventh footing and
the forest floor stays litter. Every step is put at its ground's own peak inside the band 0.45 to 0.92. The strokes
and the landings are M1.5c's still. `Engine/tools/EarthGame.Sounds` writes the sounds as WAV files with a blind set.

**Tested, and how.**

- `dotnet test`: 753 passed. `FootstepImpactTests` on the numbers an ear would give, measured again on the rendered
  files with the same rulers: the forefoot's strike stands at 1.8 to 17 times the heel's tail before it on every ground
  and variant; a rock step's tail correlates with its ring's period at 0.78 where sand's is 0.05 or under; a heath step
  has 18 to 22 sharp transients and a sand step none or one; the splash's energy is 95 % passed at 0.31 s where the
  longest dry step's (heath) is at 0.27 s, and the wash is heard at a fifth of a second; sand's share of energy above
  1.4 kHz is 0.30 to 0.35 against rock's 0.36, and soil's 0.005 against grass's 0.14. M1.5c's tests stand, with one
  ruler changed: its "roughness" (the mean step from sample to sample) read a ringing rock as smooth, so the claim
  sand-duller-than-rock is now held by that share of energy above 1.4 kHz.
- **Sabotage:** heath's snaps taken out (`Snaps = 0`): the heath step showed 1 transient and the snaps test was red;
  the file put back byte for byte and the ten footstep tests green again.
- The Unity-shaped compile green. The controls scenario on the M1.5j harness build (f9c8f3a79+dirty, built
  2026-09-22T05:17:27Z): 44 checks as a development game and 40 as a plain game, none failed, no errors, no
  corrections (`Artefacts/frames/controls-20260922T051803Z`, `controls-20260922T051840Z`); the development run's own
  record names the grounds its feet fell on as `grass:21,heath:4`.
- **The blind set** written by the tool to `Artefacts/sounds/20260922T051631Z/blind` (seven walks lettered A to G by
  the shuffle's seed 7, the key in `key.txt`) and the seven files sent to William on 2026-09-22 without the key.

**What was found on the way.** The rulers had to be met by the grounds' numbers, not the other way round, and three
things only the numbers showed: hundreds of low-passed grains add up into slow swings a grain never made, which read
as bursts in sand's tail until the grains lost everything under 150 Hz; twigs that snapped in a thickest-at-the-strike
scatter masked one another inside the detector's ten milliseconds, so the snaps became one series across the whole
step, one every 13 ms jittered by a third; and a single-pole filter lets a quarter of the top end through, so the
grains take two.

**Open (DEBTS):** his ears on the blind set, which is what pays ruling 37's footstep row; the build that carries the
steps into his game folder, installed when his game is closed.

# Contract M1.F — The body's bars

**Status:** opened 2026-09-24 on William's request, CANON ruling 48: "can you put in (for the early versions of the game) a
way to view active metrics like thirst, hunger, stamina etc. something like a percentage bar ... just reading "thirsty" when
i get thirsty is good (since you dont have those metrics displayed to you in real life), but it makes the early versions of
the game a bit hard to play, in a way i dont like." GAME_DESIGN §40 puts physical condition, temperature and hunger and
thirst in the early interface. Owner: Claude (the main session).

## What the body has to show

- **Water** (FP.1): `Water01`, the body's water against normal; death at a loss of 15 per cent; the words at 1.5 (thirsty),
  4 (very thirsty), 7 (failing) and 11 per cent (collapsing).
- **Warmth** (FP.2): the core's temperature, normal at 37 °C, death at 28 °C; the words at 36.7 (chilly), 36 (cold), 35
  (hypothermic) and 32 °C (severely hypothermic).
- **Strength** (FP.1): the work a body with that water can do, 0 to 1 (`Hydration.CapacityOf`), what the walk's top speed is
  held to; it falls as the body dries.
- No hunger and no stamina: the game does not model them yet. Food and the body's rest are BF.7, and their bars come with it.

## What this slice promises

1. **Three bars at the bottom left**, above the diagnostic line: Water, Warmth and Strength, each a bar filled by its share
   with the whole per cent beside it. Water is 100 per cent fully watered and 0 at the lethal loss; Warmth is 100 at a
   normal core or warmer and 0 at the lethal core; Strength is the capacity itself. Each per cent is white while the body
   has no word for it, amber at the first words (thirsty, very thirsty; chilly, cold) and red at the ones that kill
   (failing, collapsing; hypothermic, severely hypothermic), so the bar and the word under the clock agree.
2. **The words stay** under the clock as they are.
3. **Nothing new on the wire.** The bars read the founder's state the client already receives (its water and its core)
   through one engine-free owner of the mapping, `BodyBars` in ClientCore, which reads the models' own constants.
4. **H hides and shows them.** They show at every start in the early versions, until William says otherwise; the key is
   in the controls asset (keyboard only, ruling 40), and the controls scenario presses it both ways.
5. **No bar before a body.** They are hidden until the first word of the founder's state arrives.

## Non-goals

A bar for hunger or stamina before the game models them; numbers anywhere else; a setting kept between starts (the one
place a player's settings would be kept is the game's own, and William's game and the runs' share it).

## How it is proved

- Tests first: `BodyBarsTests` — each bar's two ends and its words' thresholds, warmth above normal full, strength the
  capacity, the stages; a sabotage turns them red.
- The controls scenario: H hides the bars and H shows them again.
- The drink scenario: its records carry the bars' shares with the words, full at the start, the water bar falling to its
  word and rising after the drink; its frames at 1440p and 1080p show them.
- The Unity-shaped compile, the edit-mode tests, and the suite.

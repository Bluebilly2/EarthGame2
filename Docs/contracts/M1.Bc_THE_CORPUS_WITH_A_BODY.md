# Contract M1.Bc — The corpus with a body

**Status:** opened 2026-09-16 as one of the loose ends William asked for agents on ("use agents to work on the loose
ends"); closed the same day with the full corpus run on it (the exit record below); the creek mouth's water and the

the bodies did from the logs alone.

## What was found

- **No creek or stream in this world holds water.** `WorldLayers.WaterBodies` sets the water's surface to the ground
  everywhere but the sea and the mapped lakes, so the creek four metres from the wake answers "nothing to drink"; the
  nearest drinkable water is a lake 1,977 m from the wake. The path's first beat has the founder drink from "the creek
  174 m inland": that beat cannot be played until creeks carry water (DEBTS; William's decision whether they should).
- **A soak's founders will die of thirst.** The loop's flat legs are run (6.25 times the resting loss) and its named legs
  walked (3.25 times), about a quarter of the body's water a world day before sweat: "thirsty" at about a hundred
  seconds, "collapsing" near thirteen minutes, dead near the seventeenth minute of a thirty-minute soak, and again near
  the thirty-fourth. With nothing to drink in reach, that is what the corpus should record, not hide.
- **The quick corpus on `Build/Player-FP2c` was green** on every row a quick run can pass, with the body per step
  costing the server's tick about 0.02 ms on average (0.035 to 0.059 ms; the worst 95th percentile 0.12 ms) beside two
  test loops running on the machine, so those numbers are suspect until read on a quiet machine.

## What this slice promises

1. **The walker drinks when it can.** A corpus founder the server has told is thirsty looks once a second for fresh
   water standing within 25 m in the tiles it holds (`Drinking`, engine-free: the word from `Hydration`, the water where
   `WorldState.StandingWaterM` says it stands, the fresh cover code at the nearest post), leaves the loop for it
   (segment "water"), pitches the crosshair through the drink scenario's own order and presses the use key until the
   word is gone or the visit's presses are spent, writes a `drink` record, and walks on from the waypoint it was bound
   for. A death is a `died` record and the loop is walked again from the wake's approach; a cut gives a drink up.
   Every `sample` carries the water the server last told, the word, and whether the founder is off the loop for it; the
   `end` adds the drinks, the drinks done, the deaths and the lowest water.
2. **The loop passes animals.** `lay_loop.py --pass LABEL EAST NORTH [WITHIN]` lays a waypoint within a stated distance
   of a place, and `--groups <soak's server run.jsonl>` reads where a soak stood its groups up and says what to ask for;
   the loop budgets the walk to the last of N2's named legs rather than the whole lap, so a pass laid after them costs
   the ten-minute walk nothing. The loop in `Routes.WakeLoop` (1.86 km) now passes 11 m from a kangaroo mob's place and
   11 m from an oystercatcher pair's, and walks the shore, the bank, the platform and the wade the other way round, so
   N2 is owed a walk on it (this slice's corpus). `--water` lays a stand by fresh water that stands, and says how far the
   nearest is when none is in reach.
3. **The checks read the bodies.** `corpus_check.py` (new) holds the soak's records to the body's rules restated: every
   sample carries the body and the water only falls between drinks; the loss lies between the resting rate and the
   running rate with the most sweat; a thirsty walker within reach of standing fresh water drank (or the row holds
   nothing and says how far the nearest was); every drink answered Done raised the water by a press's worth; every death
   of thirst on the server carries a loss at the lethal 15% and was told to the walker. `fauna_check.py`'s tenth row
   holds that a flight was recorded and the fauna records bear it out. `join_check.py`'s mirror row leaves out the
   samples within two seconds of a founder's death, when the server itself moves them to the wake (the head developer's
   addition on review: the jump is the body's, not the netcode's).
4. **Nothing of the harness holds the body.** The corpus runs what a player runs: under the beta arc's bridge (on by
   default on the host) the cold does not kill and thirst does, and the soak says so in its verdict line.

## Non-goals

Water in creeks (the world's generation; William's decision); a loop long enough to reach a lake (a leg to the nearest
would double the lap); the walker choosing to drink from the sea (it is refused as salt and the visit ends).

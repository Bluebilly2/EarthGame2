# Contract M1.D — The developer's panel

**Status:** closed 2026-09-14, the exit record at the end; opened the same day on CANON ruling 30: "dev panel: toggleable
flight, and noclip under that; slider for animal traits like wariness, distance they run, and other traits they have;
other things that are appropriate for this panel, and other things in the future." Owner: Claude (Fable 5.1).

## What is there now (2026-09-14)

- **One key gives flight and noclip together.** The fly key (M1.5e) turns on a flight that passes through the ground as
  through the air (ruling 25), in a development game (`-eg-dev`), on a server whose rules allow it (`MovementRules.
  AllowFlight`: a SOLO game's under `-eg-dev`, a host's under `+server.dev 1`).
- **Nothing a developer sets reaches the server.** The animals' traits (M1.7a; M1.7c's distances) are the server's, and
  the client has no way to ask for a change but the wire, which carries no such message.
- **The HUD is UI Toolkit, built in code** (`HudController`), and the carrying window (Tab) already frees the cursor
  while it is open.

## What this slice promises

1. **A panel, in a development game, on its own key.** In a game started with `-eg-dev`, the panel's key (`DevPanel`
   in the controls asset, F3 at the desk, no gamepad binding) opens and closes a panel over the game, freeing the
   cursor while it is open, as the carrying window does. In any other game the key does nothing and the panel does not
   exist.
2. **Flight and noclip apart.** The panel holds a flight switch and, under it, a noclip switch that can be set only while
   flight is on. The fly key and the switch move the same state, and each shows the other's. With noclip off, the flight
   is stopped by the ground and by trunks, as the walk is, and never sinks below the ground; with it on, the flight
   passes through them as now. A flight with noclip off that is turned off over the ground lands as before.
3. **A setting the server owns travels as a message.** A new client → server message, `DevSetting` (a setting's name
   and a number), reliable; the server applies a setting it knows when it was started for development, and refuses the
   message otherwise, with the reason, as it refuses any malformed message. The protocol version moves. The first
   settings are the animals' (promise 4); the world's clock is the second (promise 5).
4. **The animals' sliders.** A section for each kind of animal, one slider per trait the server holds: how far off a
   founder sends a group running, how far it runs and how fast (M1.7c), and, as M1.7a already has them, how far from a
   founder groups are stood up and taken away. Until M1.7c lands the section holds the two M1.7a distances and says
   the rest are M1.7c's.
5. **What else a developer's hand wants now.** The world's time of day, read and set (a `DevSetting` the server
   applies to its clock, so both players see the same night); the weather now, read (the temperature, the rain, the
   wind, from `Weather.At` on the client's own copy); the founder's place and what they stand on, read; and a
   button that stands the founder back at the wake (the server's `stand`, as the host's console has it, as a
   `DevSetting` of place).
6. **Room to grow.** A setting is one row in one table on each side (its name, its kind, its range, what applies it), so
   the next one is a row, not a slice.
7. **Tested.** The `DevSetting` message round-trips and refuses an unknown name and a number that is not one; a server
   not started for development refuses it with the reason, and one started for development applies it; a flight with
   noclip off stops at the ground and at a trunk and never sinks under the ground, and with noclip on passes through
   both (`FlightTests`); the controls scenario proves the panel's key opens the panel in a development game and does
   nothing in another; and the owner's eyes on the panel, opened in the game on his left screen.

## Non-goals

A panel in a game not started for development; a setting that is saved with the world (a developer's setting lasts
the session); sliders for anything the server does not yet hold (the weather's numbers, the founder's body, which are
their own slices' to expose); the gamepad.

## How it is proved

`dotnet test` and the Unity-layer compile, the count as run; `python Tools/unity/editmode.py`; the controls scenario
(`carry.py --scenario controls`) on a fresh world with the dev flag; the owner's eyes and hands on the panel.

## Exit record (2026-09-14)

**What exists.** `DevPanelController`, on an object of its own, opened and closed by F3 (`Controls.DevPanel`) in a game
started with `-eg-dev`, with the mouse while open and the founder's hands at rest: the flight switch and, under it,
the noclip switch, both showing what the body has (`PlayerController.SetFlying`, `SetNoclip`, `Noclip`); a slider for
each setting of `DevSettings.All` and a button for its deed; the local hour to read and set; the sky now
(`Weather.At` from the seed, the region and the clock); and the founder's place. `Flight.Step` given a world is swept
by the walk's rule, skims the ground and slides along faces and trunks, and sets feet that end under a gentle rise on
the ground. `DevSettingMessage` (protocol 10) carries a name and a number; `GameServer.HandleDevSetting` takes it on a
development server (`MovementRules.AllowFlight`, now the one mark of one), holds it to the table's range and applies
it: the animals' stand-up and take-away distances (`AnimalStandUp`'s two, now numbers with defaults), the local hour on
the same day, and standing at the wake as a correction; any other server refuses it and closes. `Pong` carries the
server's clock, and `ClientRuntime` sets its own to it when it slips by more than three minutes of the world's time.
What a player notices: nothing, in a game not started for development, where the key does nothing and no panel exists.

**Tested, and how.**

- `dotnet test`: 531 passed. A flight given a world lands on a hill flown into and skims up its face, never under the
  ground, and over the flat is the flight it was; a setting round-trips, and one with no name or no number is refused
  where it is read, as is a pong whose clock is not a number; every row of the table is found by its name and holds a
  number to its range; a development server takes a setting held to its range and keeps the take-away no nearer than the
  stand-up, sets the local hour on the same day and carries its clock on the pong, stands a founder at the wake by a
  correction the client takes, and refuses a name it does not know; a server not started for development refuses every
  setting, closes, and moves nothing.
- Each new test's reason was broken once, in a way that still built, and the tests went red: a flight given a world let
  through the ground; feet under a gentle rise left there; any server taking a setting; a setting not held to its range;
  the take-away let nearer than the stand-up; the hour set on another day; the pong carrying no clock; a number that is
  not one let through a setting. The code was put back and the tests were green.
- The Unity-layer compile: 0 warnings, 0 errors. Edit-mode: 11 of 11 (the controls asset holds the panel's key, bound at
  the desk).
- The controls scenario on fresh worlds with the built player: as a development game (`Build/Player-M1D2`), 38 checks and
  none failed, among them F3 opening the panel and closing it, F taking off, the crouch held sinking through the ground
  with noclip on, and F setting the founder back on the ground; as an ordinary game (`Build/Player-M1D`, the same code
  but for the panel's own object, which an ordinary game never makes), 33 checks and none failed, F3 reaching the
  controls and no panel existing.

**Found on the way.** The first dev-flagged run hung: a GameObject holds one UIDocument, the HUD's is on the bootstrap's
object, and the panel's could not be added beside it, so its building threw and the recorder never began. The panel
now has an object of its own. And a stopped flight pointed steeply into a rise, slid along the ground's normal as along
a wall, was sent back down the rise; the ground is now skimmed, the way across kept and the fall lost, and only faces
and trunks are slid along.

**Not exercised, or not holding.**

- The owner's eyes and hands on the panel are owed: the built game with the dev flag was not opened for him, his own
  game being open on the same world when this closed.
- The noclip switch off is proved against the engine's heightfield at the flight's walking pace, whose sweep judges a
  rise by the stride and would call a hill a face at the running pace; the game's ground is PhysX's, which judges the
  surface, and a flight stopped by a trunk was not seen.
- A slider's number is what this client last sent; a second client's panel does not learn it, and no client learns the
  server's own numbers at a join. The sliders for M1.7c's traits (wariness, how far and how fast a group runs) come with
  M1.7c, as promise 4 says.
- The clock's slider is set to the hour when the panel opens and is not moved by the clock afterwards.

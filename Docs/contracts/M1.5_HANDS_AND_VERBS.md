# Contract M1.5 — The founder's hands

**Status:** drafted 2026-09-11 while M1.6a's cost work ran, on the owner's "keep working, can you be doing more?".
It opens slice by slice, each as a contract of its own: M1.5a opened the same day on the owner's "go"
(`M1.5a_VERBS_AND_CARRYING.md`, which holds what it promised and what it did), and M1.5b after it on his "continue
work" (`M1.5b_TAKING_WHAT_LIES.md`). Owner: Claude (Opus 5). The owner's
lane: the feel, with his hands on the controls (CANON rulings 12 and 18), and the frames.

The plan (§6, item 8) states M1.5 as "body and hands, the feedback checklist, Input System actions, the verb rule
with pick up / drop / inspect as server intents, the Tab window (v1's unbuilt I1 contract), wading, footsteps;
scenarios `walk` (with its cliff-edge and rock-platform segment) and `controls`". M1's exit asks a stranger to pick up
and drop a cobble and a stick with the two-button verb rule and to open the Tab window. Since M1.6a the only sticks
and cobbles in a real world are the generated ones, so picking one up is the first thing a founder does that changes
the world the creation made (CANON ruling 22: "then as they do things, as time passes, things change").

## What was found before it was designed (a survey of both repositories, 2026-09-11)

- **Input.** `InputSystem_Actions.inputactions` is Unity's template, never edited; `InputSystemSource` reads five of
  its actions, and its fallback puts crouch on Left Ctrl where the asset has C. There is no work, use, Tab or
  in-hand action, and nothing to rebind with.
- **The wire.** Protocol v5 has no intent message; the server's `HandleData` takes Ping, PlayerMove and TileRequest
  only.
- **Carrying.** An item entity has position, yaw and its fall (`ItemComponent`); nothing says who carries it, and the
  founder's body is a session's, not an entity.
- **What lies is counts.** The loose layer holds how many sticks and cobbles lie on a cell, and the client draws
  indices 0 to n−1 of each by `StandLayout`. Lowering a count would take the last one, not the one the founder
  looked at: a taking must name its index.
- **Nothing re-sends a tile.** `TileService` keeps every encoded tile and clients only pull; the region file writes a
  layer-diff count of 0 and refuses any other, and a cell's file is deleted when it holds no entity.
- **The rest.** No sound at all; the HUD has a crosshair, the clock and one line; the `walk` scenario has no cliff edge
  or rock platform; there is no `controls` scenario; the mover has a wading state but both collisions know only the
  sea.
- **v1** had the shape of the verbs: an action offers itself on the Work button (hold) or the Use button (press)
  from what is looked at and what is held, and the winner labels the crosshair (`PlayerAction`, `ActionDriver`); a
  4 m reach; the held thing in front of the camera with a 0.28 s strike; hand sway and a footstep dip; and the
  unbuilt I1 contract for the Tab window (a read-only overlay of what is carried, the world running on).

## The rule this rests on (CANON, 2026-08-26)

"What an action is comes from what you are looking at and what is in your hand… Hold left mouse = work on it; right
mouse = use it; Tab = carrying; wheel or 1–9 = what is in hand." Every verb is an intent the server commits: the
client proposes, the server checks reach and what is there, and everyone sees the result.

## The slices, in order

**M1.5a — The verb rule and carrying.** (As drafted; its own contract chose a carried thing out of the world over
carrying as a component, and the generated things' address waits for M1.5b.)
1. The actions asset rewritten to the rule (move, look, jump, sprint, crouch, work, use, Tab, wheel and 1–9, Escape),
   the one owner of every binding; the fallback reads the asset; the scripted seam gains the new actions.
2. An intent message (protocol v6, ARCHITECTURE §10): a verb and its target, an entity or a generated thing's address
   (cell, kind, index). The server checks the reach (4 m, from v1) and that the target is there, then commits.
3. Carrying as a component: an item carried names its carrier, is out of the world while carried, and is saved and
   digested with it (region file version 2); Standard death puts it where the founder fell.
4. Pick up (use, empty-handed, looking at a thing) and put down (use, holding it, looking at the ground), for item
   entities; the crosshair names the winning verb ("pick up the stick"); inspect is the name and condition of what is
   looked at, on that line.
5. The Tab window (I1's read-only overlay: what is carried, name and mass; the world runs on) and the wheel and 1–9
   choosing what is in hand.
6. The thing in hand drawn in front of the camera, a stick from `StandForms` as the ground draws it (paying DEBTS'
   "a stick lying and a stick dropped are drawn by two owners"; `Stick.prefab` goes), and a falling item interpolated
   between states (paying "a falling thing is drawn in twenty steps a second").

**M1.5b — Taking what lies.** A generated stick or cobble picked up becomes a carried item and leaves the loose layer
by its index: a taken mask per cell and kind, saved as the region file's layer diffs; the server re-sends the changed
cells to every client holding the tile; the client draws a cell less what was taken. A cobble becomes an item of the
stone the stone layer names there. `stand_check` and `tile_check` learn the diff.

**M1.5c — Hands and feel.** The plan's feedback checklist (§4.8): head bob, hand sway, the strike on use, the footstep
dip; footsteps sounded by what covers the ground underfoot, made in code rather than recorded, muted in every
automated run. The feel is the owner's, with his hands.

**M1.5d — Wading and the walk.** The water's depth in both collisions (the server's heightfield and the client's
PhysX), so a founder wades in a lake or a creek as in the sea; the `walk` scenario laid again with a cliff edge and a
rock platform (CANON ruling 13), paying the divergence debt's measurement there; the `controls` scenario from v1's
K1c.

## Non-goals

Work verbs (knapping, cutting, stripping bark: M2); the tablet's page (M1.9); rebinding in a settings page (M1.9);
fibre and driftwood as things that lie (the rest of M1.6); trees felled; freeform building; a body the founder sees
when he looks down.

## How it is proved

Each slice: `dotnet test` and the Unity-layer compile, the count as run; engine tests of every intent's acceptance
and refusal; a two-client test that one founder's taking is seen by the other and survives a save and a reload with
an identical digest; the gates it touches green; frames at 1440p and 1080p; the owner's hands on the feel.

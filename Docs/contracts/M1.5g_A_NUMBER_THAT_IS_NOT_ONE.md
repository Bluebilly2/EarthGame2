# Contract M1.5g — A number that is not one

**Status:** closed 2026-09-14, the exit record at the end; opened the same day, on William's "continue", the last of
the bug hunt's findings of 2026-09-13 that touch the server. Owner: Claude (Fable 5.1).

## What was found (2026-09-13)

- **A NaN passes every check.** A move's wish and facing and a put-down's point are read off the wire as numbers and
  checked by comparison: within reach, inside the region, above the ground. A comparison with a NaN is always false,
  so a NaN passes them all. A put-down at height NaN re-enters the world as a thing that never lands, sent again to
  everyone within 1,500 m every tick, saved that way and falling still after every load; a facing of NaN is kept,
  sent to the other players and saved.
- **Only a broken client can send one.** The game's own put-down point comes from a ray against the ground, and its
  facing from the mouse; the body's numbers were already refused by the validator. This is the wire's door against a
  client that is not the game's.

## What this slice promises

1. **A move whose wish or facing is not a number is refused** where it is read, as any message the reader cannot make
   sense of is: the connection is refused with the reason, and nothing of the move is kept.
2. **A put-down whose point is not a number is refused** the same way, before the point is taken for a place.

## Non-goals

The body's numbers, which the validator already refuses with a correction; the server's own messages, which the client
trusts; numbers in a save.

## How it is proved

`dotnet test`, each new test's reason broken once; the Unity-layer compile.

## Exit record (2026-09-14)

**What exists.** `BodyWire.Finite` says whether a number off the wire is one. `PlayerMoveMessage.Read` refuses a
move whose wish or facing is not, and `IntentMessage.Read` a put-down whose point is not, each with a
`ProtocolException` the server answers as it answers any malformed message: the connection refused with the reason.
What a player notices: nothing, unless their copy of the game is not the game's, in which case it is told why it was
let go of.

**Tested, and how.**

- `dotnet test`: 521 passed. A move with a wish east of NaN, a wish north of infinity, a yaw of NaN or a pitch of minus
  infinity, written byte by byte round a whole body, is refused by the reader; so is a put-down with an east of NaN, a
  height of NaN or a north of infinity. The round trips of ordinary moves and put-downs stand.
- Each new test's reason was broken once and the tests went red: every number counted as one (both tests); the facing
  refused only when yaw and pitch were both not numbers.
- The Unity-layer compile: 0 warnings, 0 errors.

**Not exercised, or not holding.**

- The refusal was not sent over the wire from a client made to send a NaN; the reader's refusal and the server's
  answer to a `ProtocolException` are each held by their own tests, and joined by the code between them.
- The body's own numbers still reach the validator, which refuses them with a correction rather than the door; the two
  doors are left as they were.
